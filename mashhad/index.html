Arabic streaming · Android concept

A name that is a scene.

مَشهد (Mashhad) means “scene” — the unit of a film, the thing you replay, and the word Arabic critics use for a striking moment. It is short, native in every dialect, and says watching without borrowing an English word. The diacritic is kept on purpose: the fatha over the meem is set in mint, a small slanted stroke that doubles as a projector beam falling on the first letter.

Considered and set aside: سِتارة (curtain, too theatrical) and عَرض (screening, too generic in search).

The Poster, as a connected prototype

Android phone, 393 × 852 dp, fully RTL. Tap anything: it is wired with sample data and keeps its state while you move around.

Jump to a screen

Try

  • Tap شاشة البداية to replay the launch: the bar tracks real work (fonts, then each key-art render).
  • Swipe the hero dots, then + قائمتي to save; the My list badge updates.
  • Search “صحراء”, “كريم” or “بحر”, then combine with a genre chip and the film/series switch.
  • Open رمال الكتمان, change seasons, download an episode and watch the ring fill.
  • Play: the phone turns landscape. Scrub, skip ±10, turn on Arabic or English subtitles, jump episodes.
  • Delete a download or sign out: destructive steps confirm inline in rose.

Direction

Cinema-poster hierarchy: Aref Ruqaa for titles and quotes, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for interface text, Reem Kufi for numerals and kickers. Digits are Arabic-Indic. Layout, back arrows and chevrons are mirrored; the playback cluster and seek bar stay left-to-right, following Android’s guidance that media time is not mirrored.
